## What are you thinking of building?

Groundhog Vault is an autonomous treasury guardian that remembers previous DeFi failures across fresh sessions. In a controlled experiment, two identical treasury agents face the same risks: one retrieves persistent Sibyl memory, while the other starts without history. Past incidents become reusable risk policies that change future allocations and preserve capital. We plan to explore Base for constrained onchain treasury actions and Virtuals for agent coordination.
